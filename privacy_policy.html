🔒 Privacy Policy

Last Updated: January 2025

Important Notice: This extension respects your privacy. All data is stored locally on your device. We do not collect, transmit, or store your personal information.

1. Information Collection

Information We Collect:

  • Words You Add: Words you actively add to your word list
  • Learning Progress: Words you mark as mastered
  • Usage Statistics: Frequency of words appearing on web pages

Information We Do NOT Collect:

  • Your browsing history
  • Web page content
  • Personal information (name, email, address, etc.)
  • Data from other websites

2. Data Storage

Local Storage

All data is stored in your Chrome browser's local storage:

  • chrome.storage.local: Stores your word list, learning progress, and statistics
  • Data Location: Your local device only, never uploaded to any server
  • Data Security: Only you and your device can access it

Default Word List

The extension includes a preset word list (words.json) containing common vocabulary words. This is public data and contains no personal information.

3. Data Usage

We use the collected data only for:

  • Highlighting words you're learning on web pages
  • Tracking your learning progress
  • Providing word frequency statistics
  • Improving extension functionality

4. Data Sharing

We Do NOT:

  • Sell your data to third parties
  • Share your information with advertisers
  • Use data for commercial purposes
  • Send data to external servers

5. Permissions Explained

The extension requires the following permissions to function properly:

  • storage: Store your word list and learning progress
  • activeTab: Execute scripts in the current active tab
  • scripting: Inject content scripts for word highlighting
  • tabs: Access tab information for proper functionality
  • contextMenus: Create right-click menu for adding words
  • host_permissions: Highlight words on web pages (HTTP/HTTPS sites only)

6. Data Security

We take the following measures to protect your data:

  • All data is stored locally on your device only
  • No network connections are made to transmit data
  • Chrome's secure storage API is used
  • Unnecessary debug information is regularly cleaned

7. Your Rights

You have the following rights:

  • View Data: View all stored data through the extension interface
  • Delete Data: Delete words or clear all data at any time
  • Export Data: Export your word list to a file
  • Stop Using: Uninstalling the extension will delete all data

8. Data Retention

Your data is retained locally until:

  • You manually delete it
  • You uninstall the extension
  • You clear your browser data

9. Children's Privacy

This extension is not specifically targeted at children under 13. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us.

10. Privacy Policy Updates

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any significant changes will be communicated through extension updates. We recommend reviewing this policy periodically.

Contact Us

If you have any questions or suggestions about this privacy policy, please contact us through:

  • Leave a comment on the Chrome Web Store extension page
  • Submit an issue on the GitHub project page

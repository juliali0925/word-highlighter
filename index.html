Features

Everything you need to build vocabulary

No flashcard app required. Word Highlighter turns your existing reading habit into a learning system.

◆

Auto-highlight on every page

Your vocabulary words are highlighted the moment a page loads — yellow for words to learn, green for mastered. Zero interaction required.

▦

Frequency tracking and context

See how often each word appears across your browsing sessions. Read the full sentence it appeared in — real-world context is the fastest path to retention.

✓

Mastered word management

Mark words you've learned to move them out of your active list. Export your mastered word list anytime. Progress persists across sessions via local storage.

⊕

Right-click to add any word

Encounter a word while reading? Right-click and add it to your vocabulary list in one step. Build a personalized word set without leaving the page.

■

Built-in definitions

Word Highlighter fetches definitions automatically from multiple dictionary APIs with local caching as fallback. Fewer "no definition found" results.

○

Site whitelist control

Prefer to learn only on news or educational sites? Enable whitelist mode and choose exactly where highlighting appears. Easy to toggle from the popup.

How it works

Three steps. No learning curve.

1

Install the extension

Add Word Highlighter to Chrome from the Web Store. It activates immediately — no configuration, no account, no form to fill out.

2

Browse normally

Read news, articles, or any website you already visit. Your vocabulary words highlight automatically in the background.

3

Track and master

Open the stats view to see which words appear most. Mark words mastered as your confidence grows. Your list adapts with you.

Privacy

Your data never leaves your device

All word lists, statistics, and mastered words are stored in Chrome's local storage. Word Highlighter does not contact external servers with your data. No tracking. No analytics. No account.
